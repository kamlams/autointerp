## Explanations

**7108** (density 20.170%, det 0.675, fuzz 0.44999999999999996) — *sequence-initial context feature*: This dense feature fires on many tokens, but almost always in the first ~15 positions of a sequence, especially positions 1–3, and only weakly or not at all later. Strong firing is driven by early position rather than word identity, with a mild boost for short common nouns/pronouns and words following determiners/prepositions. It does not fire on late-position tokens even when they are frequent, and the first token of a sequence is also relatively suppressed.

**21663** (density 14.167%, det 0.7250000000000001, fuzz 0.525) — *sequence-initial function-word burst*: This feature fires on tokens near the start of a sequence, especially positions 1–3 and the first ~15 tokens, with a strong preference for function words like "the", "of", "that", "for", "a", and "your"; the first token itself usually does not fire. Strength is highest for early consecutive tokens and decays with position, so later firings are mostly weak, isolated function words. It stays silent on later content words and on tokens after position ~15 except occasional function-word spillover.

**31356** (density 0.826%, det 0.7, fuzz 0.525) — *narrative storytelling*: This feature fires on tokens in narrative or literary prose, especially near the start of a sequence and at sentence/clause boundaries such as commas, periods, and conjunctions. It fires strongly on salient content words—action/emotion verbs, character names, temporal adverbs—and on the punctuation that structures the story, while firing weakly on auxiliary verbs and common function words. It does not fire in technical, expository, or non-narrative text, and rarely on the first token of a sequence.

**22384** (density 0.816%, det 0.625, fuzz 0.725) — *Prepositional phrase heads*: This feature fires on common prepositions that head a prepositional phrase, especially when the PP is selected by a preceding verb, noun, or particle and is followed by a full, concrete noun phrase.
